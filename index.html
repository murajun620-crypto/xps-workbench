Help

  1. Open a CSV, TSV, TXT, XLSX, or VAMAS spectrum. Use Region to choose a measurement or Excel sheet.
  2. Enter an energy offset, or enter the measured and reference energies of a known peak.
  3. Choose a background method and an analysis range. Shirley and Linear use the intensities at the range endpoints.
  4. Add peaks, set their initial centers, widths, and heights, then select Run fit. Use Constraints to fix values, set limits, or adjust the pseudo-Voigt Lorentzian fraction.
  5. Inspect the residuals and components. Export a CSV to reopen the analysis, or export a Plot Launcher XPS Fit project.

Pseudo-Voigt combines Gaussian and Lorentzian profiles of equal FWHM. Areas integrate the full component profile. Residuals are measured intensity minus the total fit.
